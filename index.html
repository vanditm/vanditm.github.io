Background

I'm a co-founder at Floindex Systems, a Bengaluru-based infra startup building modern products and services for the future of urban infrastructure.

Previously, I worked at American Express (NYC), where I owned the end-to-end digital product experience for the Consumer App (mobile and web) and supported strategic investments for Amex's consumer business. Before that, I worked across fintechs including Yodlee, Brigit, and Kapitaltech, in data and software engineering roles.

I hold a Master's in Tech Management from NYU and have completed Infrastructure Management at ISB. During my academic years, I co-founded a multi-city student-run philanthropy initiative and co-designed a community fridge project in New York.

I enjoy geeking out on data, product, and behavioral economics—and I train seriously for endurance races in running and cycling.
